How to play

• Match the top card by colour or symbol.
• Draw a card if you cannot play.
• Skip and Reverse make the other player lose a turn in this 2-player version.
• Draw Two makes the opponent draw 2 and lose a turn.
• Wild lets you choose a colour. Wild +4 changes colour and makes the opponent draw 4.
• When you have 1 card left, press the LAST CARD button fast or draw 2 penalty cards!
• First player to empty their hand wins the round.
